Lumen Rush: Data Deletion

Lumen Rush (published by O-Vortex Studio) has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. We do not store any of your data on our servers.

1. Game progress (on your device only)

Your levels, stars, coins, upgrades, skins and settings are saved only on your device. To delete them, do one of the following:

  • Open Lumen Rush › Settings › Reset progress
  • Uninstall the App
  • Go to Android Settings › Apps › Lumen Rush › Storage › Clear storage

Deleted progress cannot be recovered.

2. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. To delete or reset this data, go to Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are managed by your Google account. Your purchase history is kept by Google Play and can be viewed at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
