Lumen Rush: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Lumen Rush ("the App"), published by O-Vortex Studio ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Lumen Rush has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play. These Google services may collect some information as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress on your device: unlocked levels, stars, scores, coins, upgrades, skins, settings (sound, music, vibration), a list of purchased items, and a daily counter of reward videos watched. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or use "Reset progress" in Settings (purchased items are kept so you do not lose them).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show banner ads, ads between levels and optional reward videos. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped)
  • diagnostic and crash information related to ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown, and you can choose whether to allow them.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (coin packs, premium skins, Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. The App only receives the product purchased and a purchase confirmation from Google Play, so it can give you your item and restore it if you reinstall the App.

Google Play Terms of Service: play.google.com/about/play-terms

5. How information is used

Information collected by the third-party services above is used to:

  • show ads and reward videos, and limit how often they appear
  • measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • process and restore purchases

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect any information ourselves.

6. Data sharing

We do not share any data, because we do not collect any. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is handled under Google's own privacy policy (links above).

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the App or reset your progress. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. To limit ad tracking, reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. For more details, see our Data Deletion page.

8. Children's privacy

Lumen Rush is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. Communication with Google services uses secure, encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
